# Controls — Medieval Blood Arena

## Desktop Controls (Default — Rebindable in Settings / Controls)

| Action | Default Input | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` | Acceleration-based physical locomotion relative to camera |
| Camera Look | `Mouse` (Pointer Lock in combat) | Smooth camera orbit and fighter turning |
| Direct Weapon Control & Swing | `Left Mouse Button` (Hold + Move, or Quick Tap) | Hold + Move locks camera and steers hand & weapon directly with natural post-release follow-through; quick click triggers a crisp slash; double-tap or `F` to thrust |
| Free Weapon Aim / Stance | `Left Alt` (Hold) or `V` (Toggle) + `Mouse` | Locks camera for smooth slow/precise weapon placement without attacking |
| Rotate Blade Edge / Wrist | `Mouse Wheel` or `Middle Mouse` (Hold + Move) | Smoothly rotates wrist roll and weapon pitch directly |
| Defensive Guard / Parry Angle | `Right Mouse Button` (Hold + Move) | Raises shield or weapon guard and lets you angle the block high/low/left/right |
| Left Hand Grab / Release | `Q` | Toggle off-hand grip, grab nearby weapon/shield, or punch |
| Right Hand Grab / Release | `E` | Toggle primary-hand grip, pick up dropped weapon, or swap |
| Jump / Lunge Step | `Space` | Upward leg impulse / forward lunge step |
| Recover / Reset | `R` | Pick up nearest dropped weapon in combat, or reset Training session |
| Pause Menu | `Tab` or `Esc` | Pause simulation and open pause menu |

## Mobile & Touch Controls
- **Left Virtual Joystick**: Smooth analog speed control (`idle` → `walk` → `run`) with radial deadzone and instant zero on release.
- **Right Touch Area**: Drag to rotate camera smoothly (or steer free weapon position when **Aim** is toggled on).
- **STRIKE Button (Hold & Drag)**: Drag thumb directly on STRIKE to steer slashes horizontally/vertically with zero camera conflict; double-tap for thrust.
- **GUARD Button (Hold & Drag)**: Raise shield/parry and drag thumb to angle defense.
- **L-Hand / R-Hand / Jump**: Grab, release, punch, or lunge.
